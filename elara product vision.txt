Product Vision — AI Executive Assistant
OS
Product Name: ELARA
One-line vision
Build the operating system for Executive Assistants — one intelligent workspace that
captures everything, understands everything, remembers everything, and helps execute
everything.
The product should eventually cover the EA’s workflow from:
Inbox → Calendar → Meetings → Tasks → People → Documents → Travel → Follow-ups
→ Research → Reporting → Executive Briefings
with an LLM sitting across the entire system.

MVP — The AI EA Workspace
Goal: Build the smallest version that genuinely makes an EA’s daily work easier.
The MVP should focus heavily on meetings, communication, tasks, calendar, and executive
memory.
1. AI Dashboard
The home screen answers:
“What do I need to know and do today?”
Show:
● Today’s meetings
● Upcoming meetings
● Important emails
● Pending tasks

● Overdue tasks
● Follow-ups
● Upcoming deadlines
● Items requiring executive attention
● AI-generated daily briefing
Example:

6 meetings today
3 emails require attention
2 overdue follow-ups
1 document awaiting signature
4 tasks due today
Priority: ABC Ltd meeting at 2 PM.

V2 — Autonomous EA
This is where the product becomes much more powerful.
The EA stops being someone who manually operates software and becomes someone who
supervises an AI that operates software.
1. AI Workflow Automation
Create workflows such as:
When a meeting ends → transcribe → summarize → extract tasks → assign tasks → draft
follow-up → update CRM → schedule follow-up.
One event triggers an entire workflow.

2. Proactive AI
Instead of waiting for instructions:
“Your 2 PM meeting has no agenda.”
“John hasn’t responded to the proposal.”
“The contract expires in 14 days.”
“Your executive has three meetings tomorrow but hasn’t reviewed the briefing documents.”
“There’s a scheduling conflict next Tuesday.”
The AI continuously watches the workspace.

3. Communication Agent
AI handles routine communication.
For example:
“Ask everyone attending tomorrow’s meeting to confirm.”
The agent:
1. Finds attendees.
2. Determines communication channel.
3. Drafts messages.
4. Shows them for approval.
5. Sends them.
6. Tracks responses.
Eventually, configurable levels:
Draft only → Ask before sending → Automatically send

4. Meeting Agent
The meeting agent becomes almost completely autonomous.
Before:
Prepare briefing
During:
Transcribe + understand
After:
Minutes → tasks → follow-ups → updates
The entire meeting lifecycle becomes one automated workflow.

5. Executive Memory Graph
Instead of simple database records, create a connected knowledge graph:

│ ├── Meetings
│ └── Conversations
│
├── Projects
│ ├── Documents
│ ├── Tasks

│

The system can connect information across emails, meetings, documents, tasks and people.

6. Personal Executive Rules Engine
The EA can teach the system rules:
Never schedule meetings before 9 AM.
Always give the CEO 30 minutes between meetings.
Don’t accept meetings from unknown people without approval.
John should always be copied on ABC-related emails.
Every Monday morning, prepare the weekly executive briefing.
These become permanent automation rules.

7. Voice Assistant
The EA should eventually be able to speak to the system:
“I’m leaving the office. Tell me everything I need to know for tomorrow.”
or:
“Remind me what happened with the ABC meeting.”
or:
“Send John a message asking for the proposal.”

The north-star workflow
Everything in the product should ultimately feed this loop:
Information comes in → AI understands it → AI connects it to existing context → AI
decides what needs attention → AI prepares or executes the work → AI tracks the result
→ AI remembers it.
That is what makes this much more interesting than building another Notion + calendar + AI
chatbot.
The killer feature is ultimately:
“Don’t just tell me what I need to do. Handle everything you can, and bring me only what
requires my judgment.”
